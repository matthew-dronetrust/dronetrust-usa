# Defining Your Problem and Solution

Every successful business solves a problem. If you cannot clearly articulate the problem your drone business addresses and the solution you provide, you do not yet have a viable business idea. This is the most critical part of your business plan.

## The Problem-Solution Framework

Start with two fundamental questions: What is the primary issue that your customers are facing? And how are they currently solving this issue?

If you can identify a problem that is either unsolved or solved in an expensive or inefficient way, you have a business opportunity. Drones often replace expensive traditional solutions — aerial photography and videography used to require airplanes or helicopters, which was prohibitively expensive for most applications.

## How to Identify Your Problem

Talk to your potential customers directly. If you want to serve realtors, sit down with a few and ask them where they struggle. What takes too much time? What costs too much? What would make their listings stand out?

> **Tip:** Do not try to sell during these initial conversations. Listen first. Ask questions. Understand their pain points. The business opportunity will become clear from what they tell you.

Be realistic in your assessment. You might have an idea that sounds brilliant in your head, but if potential customers do not respond enthusiastically when you pitch it, it may not be as viable as you think. It is better to discover this early than after investing thousands of dollars.

## Example: Real Estate Photography

**Problem:** The overall location and appeal of a property is often hard to convey through ground-level photography alone, especially for larger properties with acreage or unique features.

**Solution:** Offer aerial photography and videography that provides a bird's-eye view of the property, shows boundaries, and highlights features that are invisible from the ground.

This is a simple, clear problem-solution pairing. Your problem and solution should be equally straightforward.

## Assignment 1

Describe the problem you are trying to solve and the solution you are using to solve it. Be specific and realistic. Write this in your business plan template.

## Key Takeaways

- Every business must solve a clear problem with a clear solution
- Talk to potential customers to validate your idea before investing
- Drones often replace expensive traditional methods — that is your value proposition
- Be realistic — if the idea does not resonate with potential customers, be willing to pivot
