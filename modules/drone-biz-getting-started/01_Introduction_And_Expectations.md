# Introduction and Expectations

Welcome to the *How to Start a Drone Business* course. This course is designed to take you from the idea stage all the way through launching and running a successful drone business. Whether you already have a drone and are wondering how to turn it into a revenue stream, or you are planning ahead before making the leap, this course will give you the tools, frameworks, and practical assignments to build a real business on a solid foundation.

## What You Will Learn

Throughout this course, you will learn how to research drone business opportunities, answer critical questions before you start, and create a comprehensive business plan. You will navigate legal structures and insurance, develop a marketing strategy, build essential financial documents, set up all the practical elements of your business, and manage day-to-day operations effectively.

> **Important:** This is not a passive learning experience. You will have assignments throughout the course that guide you through actually building your own business plan, step by step.

## What This Course Covers — And What It Does Not

This course will show you how to research and validate your drone business idea, help you create a business plan, explain legal structures and insurance, give you marketing and financial planning frameworks, and share practical tips from real-world business management experience.

However, this course will not paint an unrealistic picture of the drone industry. It will not teach you how to fly or how to edit photos and video — it assumes you already have (or are developing) those technical skills. It will not do the research for you, because every business situation is different.

## A Realistic Perspective

Starting a drone business is a serious undertaking. Running a small business takes significant time and effort. You will need to differentiate yourself from established competition, wear many hats (marketer, salesperson, accountant, web designer, editor, and pilot), and invest time and money before seeing significant returns.

> **Warning:** You are unlikely to make $200–$400 per hour right away, and building a million-dollar drone company overnight is not realistic. That said, do not let this discourage you — many people build successful, profitable drone businesses. You just need to go in with your eyes open.

> **Tip:** Do not skip ahead and start buying domain names or forming an LLC yet. The business plan process will help you make much better decisions about naming, structure, and strategy.

## You Do Not Need an MBA

You do not need a business degree to start something successful. You just need to go through all the steps methodically, think carefully about each decision, and be willing to put in the work. This course is designed to make that process as straightforward as possible.

## Key Takeaways

- This course combines education with hands-on business plan creation
- Running a drone business requires much more than just flying — expect to spend most of your time on business tasks
- Be realistic about your expectations, but do not give up
- Follow the course in order and complete each assignment before moving on
