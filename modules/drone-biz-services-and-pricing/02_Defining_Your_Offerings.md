# Defining Your Offerings

Now that you know how to position your business, you need to define exactly what you are selling. Will you offer individual services, bundled packages, or both? How will you handle special requests?

## Packages vs. A La Carte

Most successful drone businesses offer a combination of packages and individual services. Packages simplify the buying decision for customers and create clear tiers of value:

| Package | Includes | Best For |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Silver | 20 aerial images + 2-min video | Budget-conscious clients |
| Gold | 40 aerial images + 3-min video + virtual tour | Mid-range listings |
| A La Carte | Per-image and per-minute pricing | Custom needs |

You might also structure pricing around property characteristics — square footage for real estate, acreage for mapping, number of panels for solar inspections, or roof size for inspection services.

> **Tip:** With a la carte pricing, each individual item typically costs more than the per-unit price in a package. This incentivizes customers to choose packages while giving flexibility for unique requests.

## Handling Special Requests

Not every client will fit neatly into your packages. Have a plan for handling custom requests. Can you create a custom quote? Is there a minimum engagement fee? Being flexible shows professionalism, but make sure every job is still profitable after accounting for your full costs.

## Think About Economies of Scale

Consider how your costs change with volume. For mapping services, setting up your equipment and planning the flight takes time regardless of whether you are mapping five acres or fifty. The per-acre cost of adding more coverage decreases, so you might offer volume discounts for larger projects.

## Key Takeaways

- Offer a mix of packages and a la carte options to serve different customer needs
- Structure pricing around measurable units relevant to your service type
- Plan how to handle custom requests while protecting profitability
- Consider economies of scale when designing volume-based pricing
