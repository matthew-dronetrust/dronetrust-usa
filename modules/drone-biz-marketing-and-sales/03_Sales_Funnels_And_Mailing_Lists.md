# Sales Funnels and Mailing Lists

A sales funnel is the journey your customer takes from first hearing about you to actually paying for your service. Understanding this journey is essential for converting interest into revenue.

**Awareness** happens through Google searches, social media, referrals, ads, or seeing you fly. **Interest** is when the customer actively compares you to competitors — provide educational content here, not hard sells. **Decision** is when the customer needs a final nudge — offer a compelling deal or free consultation. **Action** is the purchase itself, but the relationship does not end here.

## Building Your Mailing List

A mailing list gives you direct access to interested prospects. Collect email addresses through your website, networking, and events. Send relevant updates and offers — but do not overdo it, or people will unsubscribe.

## Gated Content

Create valuable resources — white papers, case studies, or guides — and offer them for free in exchange for an email address. For example: "How Drones Are Helping Realtors Sell Properties 30% Faster." Do not gate content that helps people understand what you do or helps search engines find you.

## Automating Your Funnel

Use tools like MailChimp or Constant Contact to create automated email sequences. A new subscriber might receive an educational email on day two, a case study on day four, and a special offer on day six — all automatically.

> **Important:** Monitor your unsubscribe rate. If people are leaving your list, you are emailing too frequently or the content is not relevant enough.

## Sales Funnel Examples

**Online example:** Someone reads your gated white paper and provides their email. Over the next week, they receive three automated emails with helpful information. On day eight, they receive a special offer. They convert to a paying customer.

**Offline example:** You meet a roofing company at the Chamber of Commerce and exchange cards. You email them an article about drones improving inspector safety. Later, you offer a half-price trial inspection. They try your service, love it, and become a regular client.

## Key Takeaways

- A sales funnel moves customers from awareness through interest and decision to action
- Build a mailing list — it is one of your most valuable business assets
- Use gated content to attract email signups
- Automate email sequences to nurture leads without manual effort
