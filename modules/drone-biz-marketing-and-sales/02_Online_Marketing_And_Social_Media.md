# Online Marketing and Social Media

Your online presence is critical for modern business success. However, online marketing can also be a major time sink if not managed carefully.

## Social Media Strategy

You do not need to be on every platform. Pick one or two where your target customers spend time — for most drone businesses, Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube are the strongest choices.

Keys to effective social media: post consistently (two to three times per week), show your work and expertise rather than just promoting, use scheduling tools like Hootsuite or Buffer to batch-create and automate posts, and set a timer to avoid getting distracted.

> **Warning:** Social media is one of the easiest ways to waste hours. Create your content in a word processor first, then log in, post, and log out.

## Content Marketing

Create free content that demonstrates your expertise — blog posts, tutorial videos, or behind-the-scenes footage. When potential customers see you know what you are talking about, they are more likely to hire you. Content marketing also improves your search engine rankings.

> **Tip:** Always include a clear path from your free content to your paid services. A great YouTube video should end with a link to your website.

## Running Contests

Social media contests can boost followers, but be cautious. Many contest participants are looking for freebies and will never become paying customers. Focus on quality followers over quantity.

## Key Takeaways

- Focus on one or two platforms where your customers actually are
- Post consistently and use scheduling tools to save time
- Content marketing builds trust and improves search visibility
- Always include calls to action that lead back to your business
