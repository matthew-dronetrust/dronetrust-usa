# Choosing a Business Structure

Your legal structure affects personal liability, taxes, and administrative complexity. This is one of your most important decisions.

> **Warning:** This information is educational, not legal advice. Always consult a qualified attorney and tax accountant.

### Sole Proprietorship and Partnership

These offer **no personal liability protection**. If your drone damages property, creditors can seize your personal assets. Not recommended for drone businesses.

### Corporation (C-Corp and S-Corp)

Corporations provide liability protection. **C-Corps** pay corporate income tax, and owners are taxed again on dividends (double taxation). **S-Corps** are pass-through entities — profits flow to personal tax returns, avoiding double taxation. S-Corps are limited to 100 US citizen/resident shareholders.

### LLC (Limited Liability Company)

The most popular choice for small drone businesses. Provides personal liability protection with simpler administration than a corporation. Profits pass through to members' personal taxes. An LLC can elect S-Corp tax treatment for potential self-employment tax savings.

## Comparative Summary

| Feature | Sole Prop. | C-Corp | S-Corp | LLC |
|---------|-----------|--------|--------|-----|
| Liability protection | None | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Separate entity | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Corporate tax | No | Yes | No | No |
| Pass-through taxes | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Complexity | Minimal | High | Medium | Low |

## Liability Protection Limits

Even with an LLC or corporation, you are NOT protected if you cause harm through intentional, fraudulent, or illegal actions, you fail to pay taxes, you provide a personal guarantee for business debt, or you personally injure someone directly.

## Protecting Yourself

Regardless of structure: keep business and personal finances completely separate, get an EIN (Employer Identification Number) from the IRS, maintain honest business practices, and get appropriate insurance.

> **Important:** If there is no clear separation between business and personal finances, a judge can "pierce the corporate veil" and eliminate your liability protection.

## Recommendation

For most drone businesses, form an **LLC** and consider electing S-Corp tax treatment. Always consult a tax accountant for your specific situation.
