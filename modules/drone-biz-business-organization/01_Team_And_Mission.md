# Your Team and Mission Statement

Every business needs clarity about who is involved and what the business stands for. Even as a one-person operation, defining your team and mission creates focus and professionalism.

## Your Team

If you are starting solo, write a short bio highlighting your relevant experience and qualifications. If you have partners or plan to hire, describe each role, qualifications needed, and who fills it. Investors pay close attention to the team — the right people are almost as important as the right idea.

For future positions, write job descriptions now so you know what you need. Create a simple organizational chart showing the reporting structure.

## Your Mission Statement

Keep it to one or two sentences. Examples: "Aviation training made easy," "We provide high-end aerial photography and videography services for Prescott-area realtors," or "Delivering precise aerial inspection data that keeps workers safe and costs down."

Do not overthink this — keep it short, specific, and authentic.

## Key Takeaways

- Even solo operators benefit from documenting qualifications and experience
- Write job descriptions for future roles before you need to fill them
- Your mission statement should be short, specific, and genuine
