# Welcome to How to Market Your Drone Business

Most drone operators are excellent pilots and editors — and average marketers. Yet marketing is what determines whether your business grows or quietly stalls out. This course gives you a practical, repeatable system for turning your drone business into a brand customers seek out and trust.

## What You'll Learn

This course covers the four areas that matter most for marketing a small drone business:

- **Marketing foundations** — why marketing matters, identifying your real target customer, crafting a value proposition, and building a one-page marketing plan
- **Branding** — what branding actually means, choosing a name and logo, defining your voice and visual identity, and positioning yourself against the competition
- **Social media** — picking the right platforms, building content pillars, setting a sustainable cadence, and growing an audience that actually books work
- **Talking to clients** — first-contact and discovery, asking the right questions, pricing conversations and quotes, and handling objections to close

## How This Course Works

Each module is structured the same way — short lessons that explain the *why*, the *how*, and the common traps to avoid. Every module ends with a quiz to lock in the key ideas and a series of assignments designed to build the actual marketing assets you will use in your business.

By the end of the course you should walk away with a customer profile, a value proposition, a one-page marketing plan, a brand statement, a content calendar, a discovery call worksheet, a quote template, and a set of objection-handling responses. Real, usable artefacts — not theory.

## Course Structure

1. **Marketing Foundations** — Why marketing matters, target customers, value proposition, marketing plan
2. **Branding Your Drone Business** — What branding really means, naming and logo, voice and visual identity, positioning
3. **Social Media for Drone Pilots** — Platform choices, content pillars, cadence and tools, growing your audience
4. **Talking to Clients** — First contact, discovery questions, pricing and quotes, objections and closing

Each module contains four lessons plus a quiz.

## Who This Course Is For

This course is for working drone operators — Part 107 certified pilots — who already know how to fly and edit, and now want to fill their calendar with the right kind of work. It is equally useful for new operators planning their launch and experienced pilots whose marketing has plateaued.

## Let's Get Started

Marketing is the most leveraged work you do that does not require flying a drone. The pilots who treat it as a real, ongoing skill — not an afterthought — are the ones who build durable businesses. Let's build yours.
